# Research: Q2 Free Trial Push

## ICP snapshot
Marketing managers at B2B SaaS companies drowning in spreadsheet exports and Slack pings for "one more number." They own pipeline reporting but not data engineering.

## Pain points (evidence)
| Pain | Source | Quote or paraphrase |
|------|--------|---------------------|
| Manual reporting | r/marketing | "Every Monday is a nightmare pulling numbers from five tools" |
| Leadership pressure | LinkedIn posts | "CEO wants a single dashboard by Friday" |
| Tool sprawl | G2 reviews (category) | "We have the data; we don't have the story" |

## Objections
1. "We already have HubSpot reports."
2. "Setup will take months."
3. "Another subscription."

## Competitor ad patterns
| Brand | Hook style | Format | Offer | Gap we can exploit |
|-------|------------|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Generic BI | Fear / complexity | Static | Demo call | Show marketer-native UI, not SQL |
| Spreadsheets | Relatable pain | Carousel | Template | Show live product, not PDF |
| All-in-one suites | Feature dump | Video | Webinar | Trial in 2 minutes, not 2 weeks |

## Meta Ad Library notes
- **Queries used:** marketing dashboard, SaaS analytics trial
- **Patterns:** UI screen recordings, stat in first frame, founder selfie + screen

## Angle hypotheses (ranked)
### 1. Monday report pain ⭐
- **Hook direction:** "Still building the Monday deck at 8pm?"
- **Why now:** universal ritual
- **Risk:** low — no personal attributes

### 2. One dashboard for leadership
- **Hook direction:** "Your CEO asked for one number. Here it is."
- **Why now:** executive pressure
- **Risk:** medium — avoid naming real executives

### 3. 2-minute setup
- **Hook direction:** Connect HubSpot + GA4, see trials pipeline
- **Why now:** counters setup objection
- **Risk:** must not overpromise integration time

## Policy flags
- [x] None blocking
- [ ] Avoid "guaranteed" time savings percentages without source

## Recommended angles for strategy (top 3)
1. Monday report pain
2. One dashboard for leadership
3. 2-minute setup

## Raw notes
Retargeting: use social proof ("teams like yours") not competitor names.
